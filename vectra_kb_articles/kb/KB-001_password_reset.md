# Password reset and account lockout

Accounts lock automatically after **5 failed sign-in attempts** and unlock on their own after **30 minutes**.

## Self-service reset
1. Go to https://reset.vectralogistics.example from any browser.
2. Enter your employee ID (format VX#####) and verify with the code sent to your registered mobile.
3. Choose a new password: minimum 14 characters, not one of your last 5 passwords.

## If self-service fails
Raise a ticket with category "Access". The service desk will verify identity with a **video call and your company ID card** before resetting.
IT will never ask for your password or OTP over phone, chat or email.

## MFA codes not arriving
Check that your phone number on the HR portal is current. Codes expire after 5 minutes. As a fallback you can use the Authenticator app enrolled at onboarding.
