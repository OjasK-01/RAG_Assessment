# Email on mobile and mailbox issues

- Company mail on phones is supported **only in the Outlook mobile app** with Intune enrolment; native mail apps are blocked.
- Outlook not syncing: open Outlook > Settings > account > Reset account. If still failing, remove and re-add the account.
- Mailbox quota is **50 GB**. At 49 GB you get a warning; at 50 GB sending stops. Use Online Archive (enabled for all staff) to move older mail.
- Shared mailbox access requires approval from the mailbox owner; raise an Access ticket naming the mailbox.
