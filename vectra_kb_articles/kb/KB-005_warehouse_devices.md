# Warehouse handheld scanners and label printers

## Handheld scanners
- Batteries should last a full 8-hour shift. If a battery drains in under 3 hours, swap it from the charging rack and tag the old one "BAT-RETURN".
- Scanner not connecting to WMS: check it is on the "VX-WH-SCAN" wifi, not guest wifi.

## Label printers at the dock
- "Paper jam" with no visible paper: open the head, clean the gap sensor with an alcohol wipe, then hold FEED for 3 seconds to recalibrate.
- If **all** printers at a dock stop at once and dispatch is blocked, raise it as **P1**.
