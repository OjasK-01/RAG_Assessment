# Ticket priority definitions and SLA

| Priority | Definition | First response | Resolution target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Business stopped: a site, warehouse, dispatch or many users cannot work, or an active security incident | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | Team or time-critical work impacted (payroll, month-end, client deadline) | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P3 | Single user impacted, workaround exists | 4 hours | 2 business days |
| P4 | Requests and how-to questions | 1 business day | 5 business days |

**VIP rule:** tickets from leadership flagged VIP are handled as **P2 at minimum**, regardless of impact.

P1 tickets must also be phoned in to the 24x7 desk line (ext. 4357) in addition to the ticket.
